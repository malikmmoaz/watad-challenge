Market sources mix bases (mill vs dealer, delivered vs ex-works, with or without VAT), so each comparison below says which basis it uses. Sources are public pages read on 19 Nov 2026; where a figure is retail including VAT, the ex-VAT value is shown as ÷ 1.15.

## What changed (19 Nov 2026, decided by Safina)

- **Rebar repriced** to the market: benchmark SAR 2,945 / 2,920 / 2,905 per tonne (12 / 16 / 20 mm), and every sample offer scaled by the same factor (≈ 0.854, rounded to SAR 5) so offers keep their order, their spread and their price-vs-benchmark flags. Delivery fees, lead times and every other material are unchanged.
- Amounts that moved with it: the `rfq_1045` split saving is now **SAR 7,015** (single offer SAR 140,932.50, split SAR 133,917.50, incl. VAT); Rawasi's offer on `rfq_1042` is **SAR 488,169.25** incl. VAT; the supplier offer the e2e and demo type on `rfq_1044` is now SAR 2,925/t, giving **SAR 115,483.00**; the rebar 12 mm placeholder is **2945.00**; the seeded credit draw for `po_3011` is SAR 97,462.50, so the credit used and available on Najd's cap follow from it. The design-system sample table uses SAR 2,880–2,990/t.
- **Blocks** stay at 2.65 (high end, within retail). Cement, ready-mix, delivery fees and lead times stay as they are.
