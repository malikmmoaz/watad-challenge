## What to change, and what not

- **Rebar is the one clear outlier**, and it sits in almost every journey. A realistic set (ex VAT, Nov 2026) would be a benchmark of about **SAR 2,900–2,950/t** for 12–20 mm, with offers spread about ±5% around it (mill brands slightly above, imports below).
- Changing it moves amounts that tests and the walkthrough quote: the `rfq_1045` split saving (SAR 7,705), the `rfq_1042` totals (e.g. SAR 560,993.00), the supplier offer example (SAR 128,041.00), the 3445.00 placeholder, and the credit-cap figures that follow from the seeded draws. **Not changed in this PR.** The decision and the coordinated update (seed, `seed-scenarios.test.ts`, e2e amounts, walkthrough, guide) are proposed to Safina and Noor separately.
- **Blocks** are on the high side but within retail; leave them unless rebar is repriced anyway (then 2.25 ex VAT would be more typical).
- Cement, ready-mix, delivery fees and lead times stay as they are.
