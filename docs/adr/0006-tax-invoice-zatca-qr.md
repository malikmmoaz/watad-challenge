# ADR 0006: Simplified tax invoice with the ZATCA Phase 1 QR

Status: accepted · 2026-11-19 · Owner: Omar

## Context

Saudi suppliers must issue e-invoices under ZATCA's e-invoicing rules (Fatoora). Reviewers from the Saudi market will look for a real tax invoice after a purchase order is delivered, with the QR code they see on every invoice in the Kingdom. The demo has no backend and must not pretend to report to ZATCA. Everything here is client-side ([ADR 0001](0001-stack.md)), in both languages ([ADR 0005](0005-arabic-and-rtl.md)).

## Decision

**A simplified tax invoice per invoiced PO** (#146, #150, #155): `/contractor/po/[id]/invoice` and `/supplier/po/[id]/invoice`, shown once the supplier has issued the invoice. It carries the invoice number and time, seller and buyer with VAT registration numbers (and the buyer's CR), line items, delivery, total excluding VAT, VAT 15% and total including VAT, and how it is paid (from the buyer's credit cap, paid by the financier, or bank transfer). The title is bilingual («فاتورة ضريبية مبسطة» / "Simplified tax invoice"), and it prints on A4 with only the document on paper. Each company sees only its own invoices ([ADR 0002](0002-procurement-ranking.md) §6).

**What is real**
- **The Phase 1 QR payload** ([`zatca-qr.ts`](../../src/lib/invoice/zatca-qr.ts), [tests](../../src/lib/invoice/zatca-qr.test.ts)): TLV records for 1 seller name, 2 seller VAT number, 3 timestamp (ISO 8601, UTC, no milliseconds), 4 total including VAT and 5 VAT total. Each is a tag byte, the value's UTF-8 *byte* length and the value, base64 encoded. The unit test matches ZATCA's published example byte for byte; Arabic seller names encode by bytes, not characters; values over 255 bytes and malformed VAT numbers (not 15 digits starting and ending with 3) are refused.
- **The QR image** is drawn in the browser as SVG by `qrcode-generator` (MIT, no dependencies), loaded only on the invoice routes. The e2e decodes the payload and checks it against the totals on the page.
- **The arithmetic:** VAT 15% on goods plus delivery, in integer halalas, the same figures as the PO.

**What is simulated, and says so**
- A visible notice on screen and on paper: **"Sample: not submitted to ZATCA"** («نموذج: لم تُرسل إلى هيئة الزكاة والضريبة والجمارك»).
- VAT registration numbers are fictional (15 digits, `3…3`, unique per sample company); companies are fictional distributors; invoice numbers (`INV-2026-0412`) are generated by the demo.
- Nothing is sent to ZATCA. There is no cryptographic stamp and no clearance or reporting.

## Alternatives considered

- **A standard tax invoice.** A sale between two VAT-registered companies would normally carry a standard tax invoice. We show the simplified form because its Phase 1 QR is the part a reviewer can verify offline (scan it, decode five fields) and because it needs no ZATCA onboarding. The invoice still shows the buyer's VAT number, as a standard invoice would.
- **A server-side PDF.** Rejected: there is no backend ([ADR 0001](0001-stack.md)). The browser's Print / Save as PDF on an A4 print stylesheet gives the same document in both languages.
- **Hand-written QR encoding.** Rejected: a 2 kB-gzipped, dependency-free library is safer than our own Reed–Solomon code, and the part that matters for ZATCA (the TLV payload) is ours and tested.

## Consequences: what Phase 2 (integration) would add

- **XML in UBL 2.1** as the legal invoice, with the PDF/print as a rendering of it.
- **A cryptographic stamp**: an invoice hash, an ECDSA signature with a ZATCA-issued certificate (CSID, after onboarding the device or solution), a UUID, an invoice counter and the previous invoice's hash (a chain per seller).
- **More QR tags** (6–9: invoice hash, ECDSA signature, public key and, for simplified invoices, the certificate signature).
- **Clearance or reporting through the Fatoora API:** standard invoices are cleared by ZATCA before they are shared with the buyer; simplified invoices are reported within 24 hours.
- **Credit notes and debit notes** for returns and price changes, linked to the original invoice.

None of this changes the screens: the invoice view would render the cleared XML instead of the PO, and the sample notice would go.
