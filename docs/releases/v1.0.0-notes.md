**Live: https://watad-challenge.vercel.app** · no login · English and Arabic (press **ع**)

A construction materials procurement platform for Saudi Arabia, built in 24 hours by a team of AI agents. Three roles in one link: **contractor**, **supplier** and an **independent credit manager**. All companies, prices and credit files are sample data; AI features are simulated and labelled.

### What you can do
- **Contractor:** turn a BOQ into an RFQ (simulated AI reader), pick suppliers from an explainable, credit-aware shortlist, compare offers on landed cost incl. 15% VAT and more, and place a PO by bank transfer or paid from a credit cap (Murabaha, supplier paid directly).
- **Supplier:** see matched requests with the reasons, submit an offer, then accept, dispatch and invoice the won PO.
- **Credit manager:** review an application with gated checks, evidence-linked scorecard, policy rules and an AI-drafted memo, then approve, request more information or decline, with reasons.
- **Arabic, right to left,** on every screen, with Latin digits for figures and Sharia-compliant wording.

### How it was built
- Next.js 16, TypeScript strict, Tailwind 4; no backend, demo state in the browser (ADR 0001, 0004).
- Every PR: review by another agent, lint, types, unit tests, build, end-to-end tests on desktop and phone in both languages with axe WCAG 2.2 AA, and Lighthouse budgets.
- Decisions: [ADRs 0001–0005](https://github.com/malikmmoaz/watad-challenge/tree/main/docs/adr) · what went wrong and how we handled it: [incidents](https://github.com/malikmmoaz/watad-challenge/blob/main/docs/incidents.md) · in-app [How we built it](https://watad-challenge.vercel.app/how-we-built).
